No Surprises

Honest, Upfront Pricing

Before we start any repair, we find out exactly what's wrong. Our technicians check the age and tonnage of your system, the refrigerant charge, the heat exchanger and coils, and the condition of your ductwork. After that you get an honest recommendation and the full price up front, whether the job is swapping a weak capacitor or replacing the whole system. No hidden fees, no last-minute add-ons.

A lot of HVAC failures send small signals well before the system stops. Watch for rising utility bills, airflow that feels weaker than it used to, frost on the copper lines, or a bedroom that never catches up to the thermostat. Behind those symptoms are usually pinhole refrigerant leaks, worn blower bearings, or a capacitor on its way out. A quick inspection at the first sign often costs far less than a failed compressor.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most air conditioners and furnaces last 12 to 18 years, and a planned replacement is nearly always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency swap during a heat wave or cold snap. Our technicians know the climate demands common to West New York and how they affect equipment life, so we can tell you honestly whether your system has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Maintenance Plans

Maintenance Plans for West New York Homes

A West New York HVAC maintenance visit is far more than a quick filter swap. Our technician cleans the coils, verifies the refrigerant charge, tests capacitors and contactors, inspects the burners and heat exchanger, clears the condensate drain, replaces the filter, and checks static pressure in the duct system. Small problems get caught while they're inexpensive to correct, and the equipment keeps running at full efficiency.

Most homes do best with two visits a year: a spring tune-up for cooling and a fall tune-up for heating. The schedule flexes with the age of your equipment, how hard your household runs it, and local conditions like dust and pollen, which clog condenser coils and push up energy use fast.

Consider maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already bought. Each tune-up keeps the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, which lowers what you pay to run the system while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most households, the plan costs far less than an early replacement.

Right-Sized Systems

Comfort Sized to Your Home's Demand

Getting the size right matters more than most homeowners realize. Before quoting new equipment, West New York HVAC works through the details that drive your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. That gives you a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, keeps humidity in check, and holds every room at the temperature you set.

Homes change over time. A finished basement, an addition, or two new bedrooms can overwhelm the single-stage system that used to be plenty. Today's two-stage and variable-speed equipment holds tighter temperatures and uses much less energy. If you're torn between a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll lay out the real trade-offs in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Repairs We Handle

Heating and Cooling Problems We Solve in West New York

Homes in West New York run into a familiar set of heating and cooling failures, and each one calls for a different fix. West New York HVAC handles them all: no cooling, air that blows but never gets cold, furnaces that won't ignite or short-cycle, frozen evaporator coils, refrigerant leaks, condensers that hum without starting, thermostats that lose their settings, clogged condensate drains, and mini-splits flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

The right repair depends entirely on the part that failed. On cooling systems, that usually means a capacitor, contactor, or fan motor. On furnaces, it tends to be an igniter, flame sensor, or control board. Leaky ductwork bleeding conditioned air into the attic is sealed and repaired, and we recover and recharge the refrigerant once the leak itself is repaired. Some of these issues are just inconvenient, but a cracked heat exchanger or a clogged condensate drain can be a genuine safety or water-damage risk, so we treat it with urgency.
