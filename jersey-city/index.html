Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Jersey City

Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. Common HVAC Problems in Jersey City

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen.
