Signs You Need HVAC Repair in West New York

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems. Watch for these telltale signs:

  • Vents blowing warm when you want cool, or cool when you want heat
  • Water pooling around the indoor equipment, often from a clogged condensate drain
  • Frequent on-off cycling while the house never gets to the thermostat setting
  • Barely any air coming out of the vents
  • New noises such as grinding, squealing, buzzing, or banging
  • A breaker that trips whenever the system tries to run
  • An outside unit that buzzes but will not start
  • Higher energy bills with no improvement in comfort

Start with safety. HVAC Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly heating and cooling problems down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Run a vinegar flush through the condensate line each spring to prevent clogs and float switch trips
  • Trim shrubs and clear leaves and grass away from the outdoor unit so the coil gets full airflow
  • Leave every supply and return vent open and free of furniture or rugs
  • Swap in a fresh filter every one to three months; a clogged one starves the whole system of air
  • Give the system a rest during extreme weather by setting the thermostat a few degrees closer to outdoors
  • Call about unusual noises early, since a squealing bearing costs far less to fix before the motor seizes
  • Have the system professionally serviced twice a year, once before summer and once before winter
